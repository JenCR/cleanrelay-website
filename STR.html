Common Questions

Answers for Busy Hosts

Do you handle same-day turnovers? +
We do our best to accommodate same-day requests depending on cleaner availability in your area. For the best chance of same-day support, we recommend reaching out as early in the morning as possible. Scheduling in advance is always the most reliable option.
Can I request recurring turnover cleaning after every stay? +
Yes. Recurring turnover cleaning is one of the most popular options for active STR hosts. Once you're set up, we coordinate your cleanings around your booking calendar so the process is as seamless as possible.
Do cleaners bring their own supplies? +
Clean Relay connects you with independent cleaners. Supply arrangements are confirmed at booking. We recommend discussing supply preferences when you submit your request so everything is aligned before your first clean.
Can Clean Relay support multiple properties? +
Yes. If you manage multiple STR units, we can work with you to coordinate cleanings across your portfolio. Reach out through the quote form and let us know how many properties you're managing and where they're located.
Do you help with linens or restocking? +
Cleaners can assist with linen staging and basic restocking (paper goods, soap, etc.) depending on what you have available on-site. Let us know your preferences when you submit your request and we'll make sure expectations are set clearly.
How soon should I book before my next guest arrives? +
The more lead time the better 24 to 48 hours in advance is ideal for securing your preferred time slot. For peak travel weekends and holidays, we recommend booking even further out to ensure availability.
