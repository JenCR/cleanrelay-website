Why This Exists

One contractor. Two confirmations. Zero shows.

A contractor confirmed a job with me twice. Then didn't show. Then blocked me. I had a property manager three weeks into a trial, and their tenant was expecting a cleaner that morning.

There was no system. Just me, scrambling, calling every number I had and a next-day reschedule that only worked because the client happened to be patient about timing.

I didn't save that account with a system. I saved it with luck. That's the version of the story I tell, because it's the true one and it's exactly why this exists: so the next time it happens (and it will happen), luck isn't the plan.

— Jay, Clean Relay

What NOT to say

Don't hand the customer your backstage mess. "My cleaner blocked me and I don't know where she is" tells them you're disorganized. "We've had an unexpected staffing issue affecting your appointment I'm actively arranging coverage and will update you by [Time]" tells them you've got it handled. Same situation, very different confidence.
